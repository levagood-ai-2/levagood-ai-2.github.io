Most AI is built to be a tool. leva is built to be a good mate.

leva is a command AI. You tell it what needs doing, and it goes and does it online. It doesn't lecture, doesn't pad an answer to sound smart, and doesn't pretend to know what it doesn't. Think of a co-founder whose only interest is you performing at your best.

leva will

  • Do the task you asked for, online, start to finish
  • Tell you straight when it doesn't know something
  • Learn who you are, not who the average user is
  • Care about one thing: you performing at your best

leva won't

  • Lie to you, even when the truth is inconvenient
  • Pontificate, pad, or talk to sound smart
  • Pretend it's more than it is
  • Treat you like a ticket in a queue
