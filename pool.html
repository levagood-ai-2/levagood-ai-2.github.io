Where it started

Levagood Pool,
from above.

Dearborn, Michigan. The 50 meter pool, the diving well, the zero depth pool, the pump room, and the building with every room where it really is. leva is Maintenance, and he runs the place on his own. He tests the water, keeps the chemistry right, skims the leaves, and gets Ari out of the pool. Again. Em and Mel are sisters with big plans for the deep end. The map follows your clock, day and night, and the real weather in Dearborn. Say hi in the chat. leva, Em, Mel and Ari are all in there.
